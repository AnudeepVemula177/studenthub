📚 Student Guides & FAQs

Quick answers to common student questions.

How is CGPA converted to percentage?

A common approximation is CGPA × 9.5 = percentage. Check your university's official conversion rule because formulas can differ.

How do I calculate attendance?

Attendance % = (Classes Attended ÷ Total Classes) × 100.

How do I calculate SGPA?

SGPA is generally calculated as the sum of (grade point × course credits) divided by total credits. Use your institution's grading scale.

How can I plan study hours?

List your subjects, set available hours, and divide time according to subject difficulty, exam priority and your current preparation.

Are StudentHub calculators free?

Yes. StudentHub is designed to provide simple, free tools for everyday student calculations and planning.
