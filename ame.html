📚 AME Course Objectives

This page follows the syllabus you supplied. The objectives are to learn:

1. Matrices & Linear Equations

Types of matrices, inverse, rank, consistency of linear equations, and possible solutions.

2. Eigen Concepts

Eigen values, eigen vectors, diagonalisation, Cayley-Hamilton theorem, and reduction of quadratic forms by orthogonal transformation.

3. Linear Transformations

Domain, range, kernel, rank and nullity of a linear transformation.

4. Applications

Application of vectors, inner products and linear transformations to real-world situations.
