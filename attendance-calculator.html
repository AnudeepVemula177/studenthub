Attendance Calculator

A free attendance calculator for college students. Calculate your current attendance percentage from attended and total classes.

Formula / method

Attendance percentage = classes attended ÷ total classes × 100. For required-attendance planning, use the Required Attendance Classes tool on StudentHub.
